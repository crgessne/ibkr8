NEW UNDERSTANDING:
  Distance is necessary but not sufficient!
  
  The model cares about:
    ✓ How far from VWAP? (vwap_width_atr)
    ✓ Is VWAP helping or hurting? (vwap_helping)
    ✓ Recent touch to VWAP? (bars_from_vwap)
    ✓ VWAP momentum? (vwap_slope_5, vwap_slope)
    ✓ Extension velocity? (vwap_dist_delta_*)
    ✓ Volume confirmation? (vol_at_extension, vol_trend_3)
    ✓ Beyond Bollinger Bands? (bb_pct)
